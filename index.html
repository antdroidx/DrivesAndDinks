HOW TO PLAY

Players move automatically. Aim with mouse, touch, or ← →. Hold Shift with arrows for fine aim. Press SPACE or choose a shot to hit. Press P or Escape to pause. Aim rally shots and serves with the court or BALL CONTROL pad. ATTACK and SOFT become hard and soft serves.
